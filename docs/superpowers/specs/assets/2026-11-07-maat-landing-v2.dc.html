The problem

Tokenized stocks trade 24/7. Their prices don't.

An agent that only reads a DEX price will buy on a Saturday, chase a pool that drifted from fair value, or trade through a stock split. Maat turns each trap into a rule the contract enforces.

01 · MARKET HOURSMARKET_CLOSED

The price feed sleeps every weekend.

The vault reads the NYSE calendar onchain, holidays and early closes included. No session, no trade.

02 · PREMIUMPREMIUM_TOO_HIGH

The pool can drift from fair value.

Each buy compares a 30-minute Aerodrome TWAP with Chainlink. We measured these premiums in session before setting the limit.

03 · CORPORATE ACTIONSMULTIPLIER_COOLDOWN

A split quietly changes the math.

When the B20 multiplier moves, the vault records it and pauses that token for 24 hours while prices settle.

How it works

The model may advise. The contract decides.

Claude never holds the keys to the rules. It writes intents. The vault checks them against a mandate it cannot change, then emits a Decision either way.

The mandate

Rules the agent can't loosen.

The owner commits the mandate onchain. Any change is queued in public with its full contents and waits 24 hours, so neither the agent nor a stolen key can relax a limit in the moment.
